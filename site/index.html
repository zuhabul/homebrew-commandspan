How it works

Commandspan runs a daemon on your host that exposes a sub-5ms UNIX-socket bus. Agent hooks (Claude Code, Codex) journal events into it; approval requests broadcast to your phone. You decide on a Live Activity or the watch; the decision resolves the waiting hook through one shared registry — over HTTP or the bus, it lands.
